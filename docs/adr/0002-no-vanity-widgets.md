# No trophy wall, no star count, no view counter

Trophy walls, `github-readme-stats` cards and visitor counters were all removed. At zero stars and one follower they render as empty trophies and `0` — the widgets advertise the wrong number more loudly than any text could, and a future reader will be tempted to add them back once the repo "looks better".

What stays is the shape of the work rather than its score: when I commit (`productive-time`), which languages the repositories are made of, and the contribution grid. These stay true regardless of popularity.

Consequences: the page will never look impressive to someone scanning for badges, and that is the intended trade. If a project ever earns real numbers, add its card next to the project, not a global scoreboard at the top.
